Our Customer Success team is at the heart of incident.io http://incident.io. We understand customer challenges and deliver Incident Management solutions that make a real impact.

We take a customer-led approach, working closely with Product to understand and address each customer’s unique challenges. We go beyond satisfaction, aiming to delight customers at every stage and build long-term relationships based on trust, shared success, and mutual growth.

As our first Customer Success Engineer in EMEA, this is a pivotal role with a real chance to shape the team as it grows. You’ll be a strategic technical partner in the post-sales lifecycle and a trusted advisor to our most complex customers, working with CSMs to own onboarding and expansion design. As leading organizations come to rely on incident.io http://incident.io, you’ll guide governance, scalable configuration, and long-term architecture so their incident management programs scale reliably across teams, not just at go-live.
