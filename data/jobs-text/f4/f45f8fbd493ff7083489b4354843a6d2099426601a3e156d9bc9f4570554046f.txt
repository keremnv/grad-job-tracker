ABOUT THE PARTNERSHIPS TEAM

Our partners include global consultancies and systems integrators across the Big Four, MBB and specialist firms, alongside leading technology and data providers.

These relationships help us enter new accounts, support complex customer transformations and build confidence in the wider hx ecosystem. When they work well, partnerships become a force multiplier across our entire commercial organisation.

The team is still relatively young and operates with a broad remit. We work closely with Sales, Solutions, Marketing, Customer Success, Delivery and Product across joint go-to-market planning, business development, partner enablement and commercial relationships.

As Strategic Partnerships Manager, you’ll own a portfolio of key North American relationships while identifying and developing new opportunities. You’ll be accountable for turning those relationships into measurable outcomes, including partner-influenced pipeline, joint opportunities, stronger delivery capability and long-term strategic value.
