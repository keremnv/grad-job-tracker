&lt;h3&gt;&lt;strong&gt;What we do&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;

&lt;h3&gt;&lt;strong&gt;What you will be doing&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;
&lt;p&gt;We are looking for a Regulatory Affairs Specialist to join our team.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;Your role will focus on providing company-wide regulatory support to our project teams, spanning both medical and non-medical disciplines, as well as supporting the QA Director and Regulatory Affairs Manager with operational activities of the Quality Management System and related management systems. This is a great opportunity for you to gain experience in a variety of different market areas and expand and deepen your knowledge across multiple industries.&lt;/p&gt;

&lt;ul&gt;

&lt;/ul&gt;
&lt;ul&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Take initiative in identifying and monitoring key changes in relevant international regulations, guidance and standards, and interpret and communicate what they mean for both the QA team and the wider business.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;/ul&gt;
&lt;ul&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Lead communications on quality and regulatory updates and run internal improvement initiatives, owning meaningful contributions to CC’s global systems as a quality and regulatory technical lead.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;/ul&gt;
&lt;ul&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Working alongside colleagues in QA and the wider business, support the fulfilment of external audit requirements.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Strongly demonstrate and develop your leadership and project management skills to facilitate the work done by client project teams.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Apply excellent attention to detail, quality assurance expertise and a collaborative approach to process improvements and the maintenance of QMS documentation.&lt;/li&gt;
