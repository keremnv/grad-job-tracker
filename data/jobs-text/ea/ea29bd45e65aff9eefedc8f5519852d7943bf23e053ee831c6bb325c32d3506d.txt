&lt;h3&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Introduction&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;
&lt;p&gt;We&#39;re looking for an experienced embedded software engineer to take a leading technical role in the design and delivery of projects involving the Open Component Portability Interface (OpenCPI) framework. In this role you will work on defence projects, primarily in the Cyber Electromagnetic Activities (CEMA) Electronic Warfare (EW) space (e.g. &lt;a href=&quot;https://www.cambridgeconsultants.com/project/next-generation-explosive-ordnance-disposal-for-the-british-army/&quot;&gt;https://www.cambridgeconsultants.com/project/next-generation-explosive-ordnance-disposal-for-the-british-army/&lt;/a&gt;)&lt;/p&gt;

&lt;h3&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Background on Cambridge Consultants and OpenCPI&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;
&lt;p&gt;Cambridge Consultants undertakes over 300 technology, design and consulting projects every year and operates across a broad range of markets, from Consumer Devices to Industrial Systems to Defence and National Security. In the defence sector Cambridge Consultants has significant experience delivering complex OpenCPI projects and has worked on all aspects of OpenCPI development: core framework improvements; OpenCPI System Support Project (OSP) development; and component, worker, and application development. We&#39;re looking for people with relevant previous experience to help increase our OpenCPI capability and deliver these complex projects which are important to our clients&#39; ambitions.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;h3&gt;&lt;strong&gt;What you’ll be doing&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;
&lt;p&gt;We&#39;re looking for someone who appreciates doing a broad range of tasks and is keen to take on positions of leadership and responsibility to facilitate the sale and delivery of OpenCPI projects.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;Regarding project delivery, the work will primarily involve implementation, system architecture, requirements management and client interaction. Other day-to-day tasks include: mentoring and guiding junior engineers, including task definition and technical oversight; and liaising with project managers on time, budget, project progress, and project planning. There will also be technical sales work which will primarily involve high-level design and cost estimation to support commercial teams in writing bids.&lt;/p&gt;
