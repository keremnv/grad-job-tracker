&lt;h2&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Introduction&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/h2&gt;
&lt;p&gt;We are looking for a Physicist with a passion for innovation and technology to join our Applied Science team. You will be motivated by the idea of applying your theoretical knowledge and experimental skill to create real-world solutions.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;This is an exciting opportunity for a recent PhD graduate or early career researcher to tackle challenges across a range of industries—including Defence, Healthcare, and Energy—by applying your scientific understanding, and your drive and curiosity to learn new things.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;h1&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/h1&gt;
&lt;h2&gt;&lt;strong&gt;What we offer&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/h2&gt;
&lt;p&gt;This role offers a wide variety of technical challenges and opportunities for personal development. It will involve both practical and desk-based work across diverse technical areas, working within multidisciplinary teams.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;While your work will span diverse areas of optical physics and beyond, a central focus will be on designing and developing photonic integrated circuits, and the next-generation products they will enable.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;A background in integrated photonics is not a requirement for the role; there will be tailored opportunities to learn and upskill. This position offers an excellent chance to become an expert in the rapidly growing field of integrated photonics.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;We take pride in being ahead of the curve—working with and developing innovative new technology. The projects we work on are highly diverse, and you will combine your existing skills and knowledge with an appetite to rapidly identify and assimilate whatever new information is necessary to solve the problem at hand. Examples of recent work undertaken for our clients include:&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;ul&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Design and test of a photonic chip for sensing biomolecules&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Development of a novel imaging system based on multi-wavelength holography&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Teardown and reverse engineering of complex high-power laser instrument for industrial application&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Development of a miniaturised magnetic positioning system for a high-volume consumer product&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Development of a photonic chip for the projection of structured light&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;/ul&gt;
&lt;p&gt;We are a nurturing team. You will be supported to develop your career in the direction you want, with mentoring and training provided. Opportunities for development and progression are varied and will be tailored to the interests, experience, and skills of the successful candidate, with scope to develop increased technical, commercial, and/or programme expertise and leadership.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;h2&gt;&lt;strong&gt;What you bring&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/h2&gt;
&lt;p&gt;You will have research experience in a field related to optics or photonics, combined with excellent communication skills (both verbal and written) and a collaborative approach. We are looking for someone who enjoys being hands on in the lab as well as conducting desk-based analysis.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;You should have:&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;ul&gt;
&lt;li&gt;A desire to work on both on optical and photonic systems.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Practical experience of either free-space optics, fibre-optic systems, or photonic devices.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;This role will include work on technologies for defence. Therefore, you must be eligible for UK security clearance.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;/ul&gt;
&lt;p&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;In addition, you should have experience with 1 of the following:&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;ul&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Photonic design (e.g. using Ansys Lumerical)&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Fabrication and test of photonic devices&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Optical system design (e.g. using Zemax)&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Optical metasurfaces&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Numerical simulation and data analysis using scripting tools, like Python or MATLAB&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Electronics or software for experiment automation&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Practical/research experience in other areas of applied physics, e.g. magnetism, RF, ultrasound&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;/ul&gt;
