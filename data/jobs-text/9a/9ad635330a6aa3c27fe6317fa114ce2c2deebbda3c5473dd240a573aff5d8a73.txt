&lt;li&gt;Partner with senior leaders across product, engineering, ML, design, research, risk and operations, bringing an independent technical perspective that influences strategy and turns it into measurable outcomes&lt;/li&gt;

&lt;li&gt;Set and evolve best practices for experimentation, causal inference, forecasting, optimisation and decision science, choosing methods that are proportionate to the decision and its risks&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Remain hands-on where your expertise is most valuable: exploring complex data, designing analyses and experiments, building models or prototypes, and resolving the hardest technical questions&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Steward critical data products and initiatives end-to-end, ensuring they are robust, well-governed, clearly documented and adopted by the people who need them&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Balance pace and analytical rigour, making pragmatic trade-offs while protecting the quality, reproducibility and interpretability of your work&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Raise Data Science standards across Monzo through technical direction, proposals, reviews, reusable methods and thoughtful challenge&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Coach and mentor data scientists and analytics engineers, using live projects and wider discipline initiatives to build sustainable capability beyond your own individual output&lt;/li&gt;
