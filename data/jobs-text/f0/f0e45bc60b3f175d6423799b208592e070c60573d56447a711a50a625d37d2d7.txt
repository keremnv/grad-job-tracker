&lt;p&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Our Prudential Risk team⭐&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;Our Prudential Risk team is expanding, and we are searching for a Manager to join our 2nd Line Prudential Risk Oversight team. You will be part of the broader 2LoD Risk and Compliance department here at Monzo.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;This represents a fantastic chance to contribute to a scaling bank with high aspirations. By joining the Prudential Risk Oversight team, you will champion our value to &quot;grow Monzo safely.&quot; Your focus will involve collaborating with 1st Line Financial Risk to provide expert second-line oversight of their key prudential regulatory deliverables including the ICAAP, Recovery Plan and Resolution Planning. The role will also interpret and assess the impact of regulatory change and support the review and development of financial models used for financial forecasting, capital planning, and stress testing.&amp;nbsp;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;Ideally you will have had experiences producing or reviewing regulatory submissions, the role will support the Senior Manager overseeing the ICAAP, Recovery Plan and across all RAF barriers, with a key focus on the financial risk barriers. You will work alongside the first line to provide in-flight review and challenge and complete independent assurance reviews on discrete areas of focus. Part of the role will involve reviewing stress testing outputs from Anaplan for the ICAAP, Recovery Planning and Valuation In Resolution, it would be beneficial to have experience analysing large data sets in excel/g-sheets.&amp;nbsp;&amp;nbsp;&amp;nbsp;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;A successful applicant will bring hands-on experience of interpreting and challenging capital prudential regulations and have the ability to develop and build strong working relationships with colleagues across the three lines of defence.&amp;nbsp;&lt;/p&gt;
