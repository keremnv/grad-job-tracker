&lt;h3&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Engineering at Monzo&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;
&lt;p&gt;Monzo has successfully launched in Ireland and is expanding across Europe. We&#39;re now entering the next phase of our journey: building and scaling a multi-market European bank.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;The EU Engineering organisation sits at the centre of that ambition. We are responsible for helping Monzo launch, grow and operate across Europe, navigating everything from product-market fit and customer growth to regulatory requirements, localisation and operational scale.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;Unlike most engineering organisations at Monzo, EU does not own a single product area. We work horizontally across the company, partnering with teams across Payments, Core Banking, Growth, Wealth, Customer Operations, Fincrime, Platform and many others to bring Monzo&#39;s products to new markets and deliver great customer experiences locally.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;As an Engineering Director, you&#39;ll lead a distributed engineering organisation across the UK, Spain and remote locations. You&#39;ll partner closely with Product, Design, Data, Operations, Risk, Compliance and executive stakeholders to solve complex problems that span organisational boundaries.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;Success in this role comes through influence, relationships and organisational leadership as much as execution. You&#39;ll need to build alignment across a wide range of teams, create clarity in ambiguity, and help Monzo discover what works in new markets while establishing the foundations that allow us to scale across Europe.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;This is one of Monzo&#39;s most strategically important areas and a unique opportunity to help build a European bank from the ground up.&lt;/p&gt;

&lt;ol&gt;
&lt;li&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Initial Call (1 hour)&lt;/strong&gt; You&#39;ll meet with one of our senior engineering leaders. &lt;li&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Loop Stage&lt;/strong&gt; - This is one stage with 4 interviews (totalling 4 hours) split between a collaboration with one of our engineers on a technical exercise, as well as execution and leadership interviews.&lt;/li&gt;
