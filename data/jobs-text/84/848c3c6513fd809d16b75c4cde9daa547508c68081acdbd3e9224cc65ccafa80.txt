WHAT THAT LOOKS LIKE DAY TO DAY

Starting in January, we're scaling the BDR team significantly. We'll hire in cohorts, target high potential, and invest heavily in the people who show it.

We're designing a comprehensive in-house training program for these future hires, but the program won't work without a proven people manager to lead it.

Our BDRs historically go on to become our commercial and enterprise reps, but we also see an opportunity to build pathways into other GTM functions, including: rev ops, solutions engineering, expansion account management, and CS.

We view the BDR org as the backbone of our hiring system. The quality of who you bring in and how fast you develop them is the number one priority of this role.

You'll also be our ears and voice at the top of the funnel. The trends your reps hear, the objections, the pushback, all of it needs to get to sales, marketing and product. You'll hold your team to account on follow-up and be marketing's point of contact for campaigns and events.

Of course you'll also own the pipeline number for your team. We care whether you're reaching potential champions inside our ICP whose pain we can actually fix, not whether the meetings-held count looked healthy on Friday.

Last but not least, we're also rethinking what a BDR is now that AI has removed most of the constraints the role used to carry. We think the modern BDR function is made up of highly agentic, first-principles thinkers limited mainly by their own curiosity. Part of your job is finding the plays that outperform, whether they're AI-driven or just creative, and turning them into how everyone works.
