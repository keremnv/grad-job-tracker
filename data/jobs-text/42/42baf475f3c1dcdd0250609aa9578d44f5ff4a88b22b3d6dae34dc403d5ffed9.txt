THE ROLE IN ONE LINE

Customers trust us when things go wrong. We're the software reliability platform built to investigate, respond to and prevent incidents. As a Commercial CSM, you're the partner to our mid-market engineering teams - getting them onboarded, embedded and expanding their usage, and maturing their incident management and reliability as they grow.


WHAT THAT LOOKS LIKE DAY TO DAY

Our Customer Success organisation is at the heart of incident.io http://incident.io. We're the team closest to our customers' hardest problems, and we're trusted to solve them. We believe in a customer-led product approach, working hand in hand with Product to uncover and address what each customer really needs. Our goal goes beyond satisfaction: we want to delight customers at every stage of their journey, and build long-term relationships grounded in trust, shared success and mutual growth.

You'll look after 70–80 Commercial customers: growth-oriented tech companies, mostly under 1,000 people, between $30k and $150k ARR each. Industry leaders like Vanta, Monday.com http://Monday.com and Loom sit in this segment. Some are steady, some are scaling fast, and much of the job is knowing which is which, and what each one needs from you.

Most of your time goes to mid-market customers on our top-tier plan, where you're their dedicated CSM leading onboarding and adoption. You'll blend 1:1 time with scalable programmes to get teams to value quickly: best-practice sessions, end-user training, partnership and roadmap reviews. You'll also cover a subset of smaller customers in a low-touch capacity, leaning on usage data to spot risk and opportunity early.

Day to day, this is a high ownership role. You'll help customers navigate the product, unpick problems when they hit them, and keep everyone pointed at the outcomes their team actually cares about. You are responsible for proactively identifying expansion and upsell opportunities, and partnering with Sales and Expansion Account Managers to grow revenue within your accounts. Internally, you're the voice of the Commercial customer with Product, Engineering, Sales, Marketing and Support.

There's still a lot to build and a ton of opportunity for you to shape this segment. The Commercial motion works, but scaling and automating the customer journey is live work we're ramping up now - refining playbooks, automating the repeatable stuff, building the tooling and content that makes a portfolio this size easier to serve. We're AI-forward in how we approach it, so you'd be shaping this rather than inheriting it.
