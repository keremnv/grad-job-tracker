&lt;h2&gt;Introduction&lt;/h2&gt;
&lt;p&gt;We are looking for an RF Electronics Engineer to join our world-class multidisciplinary team and help solve complex technical challenges across a wide range of industries.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;h3&gt;The role&lt;/h3&gt;
&lt;p&gt;At Cambridge Consultants, we help our clients develop breakthrough technologies across industries including Energy and Industrial, Defence and Security, Consumer, Telecoms, Healthcare and Biotechnology, and many others.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;As an Electronics Engineer with an RF focus, you will work within multidisciplinary project teams to solve challenging technical problems for clients ranging from ambitious start-ups to global organisations and government bodies. You&#39;ll take ownership of the electronics elements of projects, contributing both broad electronics expertise and specialist RF knowledge.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;We&#39;re interested in hearing from engineers at different stages of their careers, from those building on an existing RF specialism through to experienced RF engineers looking to apply their expertise to a wider variety of technical challenges. We&#39;ll shape the role around the skills, experience and impact you bring.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;We&#39;re less concerned with experience of specific software packages than with a strong track record of solving RF and electronics challenges. However, candidates may have experience with tools such as CST, HFSS, Microwave Office, and Altium PCB design, and we&#39;d welcome exposure to any of these areas, even if not all of them.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;This role is well suited to engineers who enjoy tackling unfamiliar problems, working closely with clients and multidisciplinary teams, and taking ownership of delivering robust, real-world solutions. As your career develops, you&#39;ll have opportunities to deepen your technical expertise, broaden your responsibilities, and contribute to the growth of new client opportunities.&lt;/p&gt;
