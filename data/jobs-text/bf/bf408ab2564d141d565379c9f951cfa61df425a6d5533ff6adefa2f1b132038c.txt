&lt;p&gt;&lt;strong&gt;&lt;span data-contrast=&quot;auto&quot;&gt;About us&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;span data-ccp-props=&quot;{}&quot;&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;&lt;span data-contrast=&quot;none&quot;&gt;Riverlane’s&amp;nbsp;mission is to master quantum error correction (QEC) and unlock a new age of human progress. From advances in material and climate science, to complex chemistry simulation for new drug design, quantum computers will help humanity solve some of its most important challenges. But without QEC, the industry’s defining technical challenge, such breakthroughs can never be achieved.&amp;nbsp;&amp;nbsp;Riverlane is the world leader in QEC technology. QEC is a complex problem that requires a range of skills,&amp;nbsp;talent&amp;nbsp;and passion.&amp;nbsp;&lt;/span&gt;&lt;span data-ccp-props=&quot;{&amp;quot;201341983&amp;quot;:0,&amp;quot;335559740&amp;quot;:276}&quot;&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/p&gt;

&lt;p&gt;&lt;strong&gt;&lt;span data-contrast=&quot;auto&quot;&gt;About the role&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;strong&gt;&lt;span data-contrast=&quot;none&quot;&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;Riverlane delivers a growing portfolio of collaborative R&amp;amp;D projects funded by Innovate UK and other public bodies, working alongside partners across industry, academia, and national institutions. Much of this will be driven by Proqure: Scaling UK Quantum Computing, Innovate UK&#39;s flagship programme for UK quantum computing, with projects starting autumn 2026. You will own delivery of five to ten of these projects in parallel, from grant award through to final reporting, as an individual contributor with no direct reports.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;This is not an administrative coordination role. You will write the quarterly technical reports yourself, sit close to the science and engineering teams, and be trusted to represent their work accurately to funders, including regular travel to partner sites and national laboratories such as Oxford. It suits someone with sound judgement on competing deadlines, a low ego working style that earns trust with technical teams, and the range to communicate confidently from engineers through to senior leadership.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;You will work alongside some of Riverlane’s most experienced quantum scientists, with genuine scope to build deep, practical quantum knowledge on the job, even without a quantum background.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;What success looks like after 12 months:&lt;strong&gt; &lt;/strong&gt;every project in your portfolio, including those delivered through Proqure, is running to schedule, with the wider organisation consistently well informed on status, successes, and partner opportunities.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;&lt;span data-ccp-props=&quot;{}&quot;&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;&lt;strong&gt;&lt;span data-contrast=&quot;auto&quot;&gt;What you will do&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;ul&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Manage a portfolio of five to ten concurrent grant funded projects, including Innovate UK collaborative R&amp;amp;D, maintaining plans, budgets, risk registers, and partner relationships for each&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Write technical and scientific progress reports and presentations for funders as a core delivery output, translating the work of research and engineering teams into clear, accurate, and well-structured documents&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Forecast resourcing across the portfolio and balance priorities between projects, working with technical leads to anticipate demand on people and equipment, surface conflicts early, and agree trade-offs before they become delivery problems&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Run quarterly claims and financial reporting with the Finance team, ensuring full compliance with funder terms and conditions&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Coordinate consortium activities: partner meetings, collaboration agreements, technical expectations, deliverable schedules, and monitoring officer reviews&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Lead initial engagement with prospective partners to form new consortia and organise and host project workshops&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Track dependencies between grant projects and the wider company roadmap, escalating conflicts early&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Support new grant applications with project plans, work package definitions, budgets, and risk sections&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;/ul&gt;

&lt;p&gt;&lt;strong&gt;&lt;span data-contrast=&quot;auto&quot;&gt;What we need&amp;nbsp;&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;span data-ccp-props=&quot;{}&quot;&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/p&gt;

&lt;li&gt;Excellent technical writing, evidenced by a sample of your own work. You can take a conversation with a physicist or an engineer and turn it into a report a funder can assess.
