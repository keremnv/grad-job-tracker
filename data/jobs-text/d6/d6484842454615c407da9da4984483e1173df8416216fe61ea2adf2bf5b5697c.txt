ABOUT HYPEREXPONENTIAL (HX)

At hyperexponential, we’re building the agentic platform for commercial insurance underwriting.
Underwriters make decisions that shape the real world: which risks to take, how to price them, and where capital gets deployed. Those decisions can determine whether a new technology gets built, a business can grow, or a community can recover after disaster.

Yet much of the industry still makes billion-dollar decisions through fragmented systems, manual workflows and technology that wasn’t built for the complexity of modern risk.

hx is changing that. We bring together data, models, AI and human judgement so insurers can understand risk more deeply, make better decisions faster and ultimately enable the world to take more risk with confidence.

Born in London, hx is now a global team of nearly 300 people across London, New York and Warsaw. Leading global carriers and MGAs use hx to support underwriting across more than $75bn of annual commercial P&C premium. Backed by a16z, Highland Europe and Battery Ventures, we’re scaling rapidly across products, markets and geographies.

AI is changing what great work looks like, and we’re building hx around that reality. People here get real ownership, high expectations and the space to use these tools to increase the quality and ambition of what they can do.

Revenue Operations sits at the centre of how hx grows.

As we scale across products, markets and geographies, the challenge is no longer simply to make our GTM engine more efficient. It’s to build an operating system that can make a complex revenue organisation run exceptionally well today while continuously redesigning how it should work in an agentic future.

You’ll lead RevOps across Sales, Marketing and Customer, bringing together the systems, data, processes and operating rhythms that underpin the full customer journey.
