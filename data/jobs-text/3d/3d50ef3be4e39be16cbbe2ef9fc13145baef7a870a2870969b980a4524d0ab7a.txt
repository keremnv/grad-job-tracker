REVENUE ENABLEMENT LEAD

Hi, I'm Romain - SVP Global Sales at hx.

I joined hx six months ago because I saw a rare mix of things: a deeply talented team, a product that is genuinely changing how insurers make decisions, and the ambition to build something category-defining in one of the world's most important industries.

The commercial opportunity in front of us is huge, but it is also complex. We sell into a market where credibility matters, where our customers are actuaries, underwriters and senior insurance leaders, and where AI needs to create real business value, not just a better story.

That is why we are hiring for our first Revenue Enablement role. This person will help us build the operating rhythm, onboarding, materials and enablement programmes that make our global Revenue team sharper, more consistent and more credible in the field.

A note on level: we're open on seniority for this hire. What matters most is finding the right person to build this function hands-on, not matching a specific title. If your experience sits closer to a Head of function than a Lead, see our companion Head of Revenue Enablement posting for a version calibrated closer to that level.

This is not a role where you inherit a finished playbook, and it doesn't come with a team to manage. It is a role for someone who wants to build the first version properly, hands-on, working closely with Revenue leadership, and help shape how hx sells as we continue to scale.

If that sounds like you, join us in building what comes next.




ABOUT THE TEAM

hx's commercial engine is scaling rapidly, and Revenue Enablement sits at the heart of making that scale sustainable. Today, enablement happens across pockets of the organisation, Revenue leadership, RevOps, Sales Ops, Product Marketing, and Marketing, but there's no single owner, no operating cadence, and no clear system for turning commercial strategy into repeatable field execution.

This is where you come in. You'll be building hx's first dedicated Revenue Enablement function, working in close partnership with Romain and the wider commercial leadership team. Your mission is to close the gap between intent and execution: improving how our sellers are trained, equipped, motivated, and held to a consistent operating rhythm that drives revenue.

You'll take an AI-first approach to how enablement gets built at hx. That means using AI tools directly to accelerate content creation, calibrate messaging against live deal data, and build feedback loops faster than a traditional enablement function could.

In this role, you'll treat enablement as a system, not a content library. You'll get close to the customer, the field, and the behaviours that actually move deals forward. This is a builder role for someone who thrives in ambiguity and wants hands-on ownership of how a world-class commercial team operates.



Check out our Sales and Partnerships Candidate Hub https://www.hyperexponential.com/careers/hubs/sales-partnerships for a behind the scenes look at the team you would be joining!




WHAT YOU'LL BE DOING

 - Help build hx's Revenue enablement operating model, working with Romain to define scope, intake processes, prioritisation frameworks, and success metrics.

 - Contribute to the 2026 Revenue enablement roadmap, turning commercial priorities into training, content, and rhythms that improve seller effectiveness.

 - Design and run enablement programmes that strengthen qualification, discovery, deal progression, sales process adherence, Salesforce hygiene, and forecasting accuracy.

 - Build out customer insight, references, case studies, and value messaging that make sellers more credible and commercially sharp in the field.

 - Work closely with Marketing, Product Marketing, and ABM so sellers can confidently use messaging, launch materials, competitive positioning, and customer stories.

 - Build onboarding and continuous learning content with clear curriculum and measurable ramp indicators for commercial hires.

 - Use AI tools hands-on to build and iterate enablement assets and programmes, rather than relying solely on manual content production.




WHAT YOU'LL NEED TO HAVE DONE

 - Worked in Revenue enablement in a B2B SaaS, enterprise software, or complex commercial environment, with evidence you understand enablement is about changing seller behaviour, not just producing training content.

 - Built or contributed to enablement processes such as intake, content calibration, or measurement, even if not the sole owner of the full operating model.

 - Delivered work that measurably improved seller effectiveness, such as better discovery execution, faster ramp times, improved Salesforce adoption, or stronger commercial discipline in live deals.

 - Worked cross-functionally with commercial stakeholders, earning trust through pragmatic judgement and clear prioritisation rather than relying on formal authority.

 - Built or contributed to onboarding and learning content that shortened time-to-productivity for new hires.

 - Used customer insight and proof points to improve how sellers engage with prospects.

 - Been comfortable building hands-on, without a team, at least in the first phase of the role.




YOU'RE UNLIKELY TO THRIVE HERE IF

 - Your experience is mainly in content production, LMS administration, or training delivery without exposure to the operating system and structure around enablement.

 - You need an existing playbook to follow, or see enablement as separate from sales execution, commercial strategy, customer insight, and cross-functional operating cadence.

 - You prefer strategy over hands-on delivery, or prefer working independently rather than collaborating closely with Revenue leadership, Marketing, RevOps, and frontline sellers.

 - You're looking for a role that comes with people management from day one.

If reading our Culture Document https://hx.wiki/ leaves you feeling neutral rather than energized, hx may not be the place where you will do your best work. We are building something that asks for commitment and conviction, and we want you to feel excited by the opportunity to grow with us.
