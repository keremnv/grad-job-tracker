&lt;p&gt;&lt;strong&gt;About us&lt;/strong&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;Riverlane’s&amp;nbsp;mission is to master quantum error correction (QEC) and unlock a new age of human progress. From advances in material and climate science, to complex chemistry simulation for new drug design, quantum computers will help humanity solve some of its most important challenges. But without QEC, the industry’s defining technical challenge, such breakthroughs can never be achieved.&amp;nbsp;Riverlane is the world leader in QEC technology.&amp;nbsp; It’s a complex problem that requires a range of skills,&amp;nbsp;talent&amp;nbsp;and passion.&amp;nbsp;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;Having raised more than $125M in funding to date to accelerate our&amp;nbsp;cutting-edge&amp;nbsp;R&amp;amp;D in quantum error correction (QEC), Riverlane partners with many of the world’s leading quantum hardware providers and governments to make fault-tolerant quantum computing a reality.&amp;nbsp;We’re&amp;nbsp;making remarkable progress and growing fast&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;&lt;strong&gt;About the role&lt;/strong&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;We are looking for an exceptional &lt;strong&gt;Product Marketing Manager&lt;/strong&gt; to lead the positioning, messaging, and go-to-market strategy for &lt;a href=&quot;https://www.riverlane.com/deltaflow&quot;&gt;Deltaflow&lt;/a&gt;, our real-time quantum error correction (QEC) system. Deltaflow is a critical component in the quantum computing stack, enabling the ultra-fast data processing and decoding required to correct errors in quantum computers in real time with high accuracy and throughput.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;This is a unique opportunity to join a world-leading deep-tech company at a pivotal stage of market development. You will sit at the intersection of engineering, product, customers, and commercial strategy, translating highly technical innovations into compelling value propositions for quantum computing companies, national laboratories and HPC centres.&amp;nbsp; You will help us craft the value proposition and marketing plan for each of our partners.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;&lt;strong&gt;What you will do&lt;/strong&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;ul&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Oversee Deltaflow’s product marketing strategy, positioning, messaging architecture and customer value proposition(s)&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Segment the Deltaflow value proposition for different audiences, particularly quantum hardware companies, national labs and HPC Centres. They may require different capability bundles&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Develop product briefs, presentations, website content, and sales collateral&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Create effective collateral and campaigns including product website, video explainers, webinars and demos&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Conduct market and competitor research analysis and reporting&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Oversee our partner marketing programme for Deltaflow, covering both customers and technology partners. This will include joint demos, case studies, talks, usage data and testimonials&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Manage one partner marketing manager, initially&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Stay abreast of important engineering, science and product developments in the industry, particularly as it relates to QEC, real time decoding, control and simulation&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Be a key member of a collaborative Marketing team of 6-8 people&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Be a champion and voice of the customer within Riverlane&lt;/li&gt;
