&lt;h3&gt;&lt;strong&gt;&lt;span data-contrast=&quot;auto&quot;&gt;About us&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;span data-ccp-props=&quot;{}&quot;&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;
&lt;p&gt;&lt;span data-contrast=&quot;none&quot;&gt;Riverlane’s&amp;nbsp;mission is to master quantum error correction (QEC) and unlock a new age of human progress. From advances in material and climate science, to complex chemistry simulation for new drug design, quantum computers will help humanity solve some of its most important challenges. But without QEC, the industry’s defining technical challenge, such breakthroughs can never be achieved.&amp;nbsp;&amp;nbsp;Riverlane is the world leader in QEC technology. QEC is a complex problem that requires a range of skills,&amp;nbsp;talent&amp;nbsp;and passion.&amp;nbsp;&lt;/span&gt;&lt;span data-ccp-props=&quot;{&amp;quot;201341983&amp;quot;:0,&amp;quot;335559740&amp;quot;:276}&quot;&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/p&gt;

&lt;h3&gt;&lt;strong&gt;About our new Maryland office&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;
&lt;p&gt;We are opening an office in Maryland to work more closely with the US government agencies, national laboratories and research institutions that are shaping the future of quantum computing. This role is one of our founding hires there. You will help establish Riverlane&#39;s presence in one of the densest concentrations of quantum research in the world, and have a direct hand in how we build the team, the partnerships and the programs that follow.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;h3&gt;&lt;strong&gt;About the role&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;
&lt;p&gt;As a Lead Quantum Scientist, Government Programs, you will take technical ownership of some of Riverlane&#39;s most strategically important research programs - collaborations with national laboratories, government agencies and major research institutions. These partnerships sit at the core of accelerating the path to utility-scale quantum computing, and require scientific depth, reliable delivery, and the ability to work credibly with sophisticated technical stakeholders.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;This is a hands-on role as much as a leadership one. You will run and shape the research yourself, act as the named technical lead or investigator on programs, own delivery against agreed milestones, and serve as the senior technical point of contact for your sponsors and partners. You will also work directly with quantum hardware partners - helping them design, run and interpret QEC experiments using Riverlane&#39;s software and hardware products, and feeding what you learn back into our research and product roadmap.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;You will operate across Riverlane&#39;s highly collaborative Research and Engineering organization, working alongside mathematicians, physicists, computer scientists, and hardware and software engineers. As the Maryland team grows, there will be opportunities to mentor and to build a small research group around your programs.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;h3&gt;&lt;strong&gt;What you will do&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;
&lt;p&gt;Your work will vary, but typical activities may include:&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;ul&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Lead the technical delivery of government-funded QEC research programs, owning milestones, deliverables and technical reporting&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Act as Riverlane&#39;s senior technical interface to government agencies, national labs and research partners&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Conceive and conduct original research in quantum error correction and related areas, working hands-on where appropriate&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Work with partners across the US with varied qubit architectures to design and execute quantum computing experiments, using Riverlane&#39;s DeltaKit software and tools&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Investigate experimental results, diagnose issues, and work with internal research and product teams to resolve them&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Lead technical contributions to competitive funding proposals and oral evaluations&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Publish high-quality research and present results at leading journals, conferences and technical forums&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Help establish Riverlane&#39;s presence in Maryland, building relationships across the local quantum research ecosystem&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Mentor researchers and contribute to Riverlane&#39;s research culture and technical excellence&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;/ul&gt;
