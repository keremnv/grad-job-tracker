&lt;p&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Build strong partnerships at every level&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;You’ll work with a large and varied group of partners. This could include frontline Operations teams, subject matter experts, product and engineering teams, risk and compliance partners, senior leaders and executives.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Drive change, not just delivery&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;You’ll help the whole collective adopt new ways of working. You’ll understand how changes affect people, processes and technology, then build that into the programme from the start.&lt;/p&gt;
