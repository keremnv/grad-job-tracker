&lt;p class=&quot;isSelectedEnd&quot;&gt;At Cambridge Consultants we are on a mission to use deep tech to transform business, society and the planet. &lt;h3&gt;&lt;strong&gt;What we can offer&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;
&lt;p class=&quot;isSelectedEnd&quot;&gt;This role involves contributing to real advisory and technology development projects for clients facing technology challenges related to net zero, climate resilience, or environmental regulations. Cambridge Consultants conducts an extraordinary breadth of projects, and you may also have the opportunity to apply your skills and environmental science expertise to projects beyond those with a core sustainability focus.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p class=&quot;isSelectedEnd&quot;&gt;Some of your responsibilities will include:&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;ul data-spread=&quot;false&quot;&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Contributing to real client projects, applying your skills to sustainability challenges across sectors such as energy transition, telecoms, healthcare, and the built environment.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Supporting analysis, prototyping, and the communication of multidisciplinary solutions that deliver measurable environmental or social impact.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Working across disciplines, collaborating with engineers, scientists, designers, and domain experts to represent environmental and social impacts.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Presenting findings and ideas clearly to both technical and non-technical audiences, including drafting content for reports, producing diagrams, and building PowerPoint presentations.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Participating in internal communities of practice and contributing to our growing knowledge base in this area.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;/ul&gt;
