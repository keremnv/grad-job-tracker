&lt;p&gt;&lt;br&gt;&lt;strong&gt;About our Data Discipline&amp;nbsp;&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;We have a strong culture of data-driven decision making across the whole company. Data is central to how we understand our customers, shape our products, manage risk and grow Monzo sustainably. We believe in empowering teams with high-quality, trusted data and self-serve analytics, while creating space for Data practitioners to focus on the highest-impact questions, opportunities and decisions.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;Our Data discipline is centralised, but deeply embedded in Monzo’s product areas. Data Scientists, Analytics Engineers, Machine Learning Engineers and Analysts work in cross-functional squads alongside Engineers, Designers, Product Managers, Marketers and other specialists. This means Data is not a downstream service: we help shape strategy, challenge thinking, build the data foundations teams need to move quickly, and share accountability for customer and commercial outcomes.&lt;/p&gt;

&lt;p&gt;The role also sits at the intersection of Monzo’s broader ambition to build truly borderless banking and expand into new markets. &lt;p&gt;You’ll partner closely with Product, Engineering, and Design to help shape Monzo’s payments strategy to make Monzo&#39;s global ambitions a reality, enabling revenue and customer growth with a resilient, self-serve banking platform.&amp;nbsp;&lt;/p&gt;
