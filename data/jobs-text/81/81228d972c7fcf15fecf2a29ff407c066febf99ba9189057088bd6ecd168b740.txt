&lt;h4&gt;Our Payments team⭐&lt;/h4&gt;
&lt;p&gt;Payments is at the heart of Monzo. It’s how our customers move, receive, spend and manage their money, from cards and bank transfers to cross-border payments, merchant acquiring, payment experiences in the app, and the platform infrastructure that underpins the whole bank.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;This role sits in our Payments Platform area, working most closely with our Finance Infrastructure squad. The squad sits in Payments, but partners deeply with Finance teams to build the systems, abstractions and capabilities Monzo needs to operate safely, scale efficiently, and expand into new markets. You’ll work on foundational financial infrastructure: the ledgers, data, controls, reporting, reconciliation and platform capabilities that help Monzo understand, account for and manage money across the bank.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;This is a role for someone who gets genuinely excited by complex, technical infrastructure. The work is often behind the scenes, but it’s business-critical: it helps Monzo scale, launch new products and markets, support Finance, and keep the bank running safely.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;h4&gt;🔑You’ll play a key role by...&lt;/h4&gt;
&lt;ul&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Defining the product strategy and roadmap for Finance Infrastructure within Payments Platform.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Partnering closely with Engineering, Finance and Payments leaders to understand what Monzo needs from its financial systems today and as we scale.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Moving the team from reactive delivery towards a more strategic, product-led approach, asking why we’re building things, what good looks like, and how today’s choices support Monzo’s long-term needs.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Translating complex finance, accounting, operational and regulatory needs into scalable platform capabilities.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Prioritising across competing stakeholder needs, balancing urgent delivery with long-term system health.&lt;/li&gt;
