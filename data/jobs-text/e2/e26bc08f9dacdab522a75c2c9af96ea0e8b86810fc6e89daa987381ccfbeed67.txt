&lt;h3&gt;&lt;strong&gt;&lt;span data-contrast=&quot;auto&quot;&gt;About us&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;span data-ccp-props=&quot;{}&quot;&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;
&lt;p&gt;&lt;span data-contrast=&quot;none&quot;&gt;Riverlane’s&amp;nbsp;mission is to master quantum error correction (QEC) and unlock a new age of human progress. From advances in material and climate science, to complex chemistry simulation for new drug design, quantum computers will help humanity solve some of its most important challenges. But without QEC, the industry’s defining technical challenge, such breakthroughs can never be achieved.&amp;nbsp;&amp;nbsp;Riverlane is the world leader in QEC technology. QEC is a complex problem that requires a range of skills,&amp;nbsp;talent&amp;nbsp;and passion.&amp;nbsp;&lt;/span&gt;&lt;span data-ccp-props=&quot;{&amp;quot;201341983&amp;quot;:0,&amp;quot;335559740&amp;quot;:276}&quot;&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/p&gt;

&lt;h3&gt;&lt;strong&gt;&lt;span data-contrast=&quot;auto&quot;&gt;About the role&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;strong&gt;&lt;span data-contrast=&quot;none&quot;&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;
&lt;p&gt;&lt;span data-ccp-props=&quot;{}&quot;&gt;Riverlane is looking for an experienced Linux administrator to own and grow our core computational infrastructure. This is a hands-on role combining day-to-day administration of our High-Performance Computing (HPC) environment with longer-term planning, automation, and improvement of our infrastructure estate.&lt;br&gt;&lt;br&gt;You&#39;ll be the primary owner of our HPC cluster and core Linux infrastructure, working closely with IT management and wider Infrastructure Team to keep systems reliable today while building towards a more resilient, scalable environment for tomorrow.&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;h3&gt;&lt;strong&gt;&lt;span data-ccp-props=&quot;{}&quot;&gt;Key Responsibilities&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;
&lt;p&gt;&lt;strong&gt;&lt;span data-contrast=&quot;auto&quot;&gt;HPC Cluster Administration&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;ul&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Administer and maintain the HPC cluster, including compute nodes, storage systems, and networking.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Evaluate and evolve cluster topology, compute node configuration, and hardware resource utilisation as the estate grows.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Lead the deployment, configuration, and troubleshooting of HPC tooling, particularly Slurm — including queue structures and job scheduling policies.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Monitor system performance and ensure high availability of HPC resources, using observability tools such as Prometheus; investigate anomalies and outages.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Assess and maintain the network file storage solution for throughput, scalability, and latency.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;/ul&gt;
&lt;p&gt;&lt;strong&gt;&lt;span data-contrast=&quot;auto&quot;&gt;Business As Usual (BAU) Administration&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;ul&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Maintain, patch, and troubleshoot core Linux infrastructure (RHEL).&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Act as an escalation point for complex technical issues raised by the Riverlane team.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Provide operational upkeep of core infrastructure, including servers, tooling, and licence managers.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Maintain Synopsys EDA tooling, including licence server management, environment configuration, and performance optimisation for electronic design automation workflows.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Provide sound technical support to users of the HPC environment.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Improve self-service capability for users, enabling routine tasks (e.g. restarting VNC sessions, resetting passwords) to be performed without engineering intervention.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;/ul&gt;
&lt;p&gt;&lt;strong&gt;&lt;span data-contrast=&quot;auto&quot;&gt;Automation &amp;amp; Enhancement&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;ul&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Automate routine tasks using scripting and infrastructure-as-code tools (Bash, Python, Ansible).&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Collaborate with the Infrastructure Team to plan and implement improvements to HPC infrastructure.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Own work packages and deliver them within agreed sprint windows.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Build and maintain documentation for systems, processes, and configurations.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Communicate effectively with stakeholders on progress and delivered improvements.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;/ul&gt;
&lt;p&gt;&lt;strong&gt;&lt;span data-contrast=&quot;auto&quot;&gt;Security, Compliance &amp;amp; Resilience&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;ul&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Ensure security and compliance across the HPC estate, including security hardening and lifecycle management.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Maintain and develop the off-site backup strategy, ensuring data integrity, disaster recovery readiness, and compliance with data retention policies.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;/ul&gt;
&lt;h3&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Requirements &amp;amp; Experience&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;
&lt;ul&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Extensive experience administering and growing infrastructure in fast-paced engineering environments.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Enterprise Linux expertise: deep knowledge of Red Hat Enterprise Linux (RHEL) administration, security hardening, lifecycle management, and troubleshooting.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Hands-on experience configuring, scaling, tuning, and troubleshooting job schedulers (e.g. Slurm) and distributed compute environments.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Strong understanding of network file storage protocols and network optimisation for computational workloads.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Proficient with container environments and virtualisation infrastructure.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;High proficiency with infrastructure automation tools (Ansible, Python, Bash) and infrastructure-as-code practices.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Experience with backup and disaster recovery strategy and implementation.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Experience managing licensed EDA or similar specialist software tooling (e.g. Synopsys) is a strong plus.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Excellent communication skills, with the ability to support both technical and non-technical users and translate complex issues into clear guidance.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;/ul&gt;
