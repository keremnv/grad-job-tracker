&lt;p&gt;&lt;span data-contrast=&quot;none&quot;&gt;Riverlane’s&amp;nbsp;mission is to master quantum error correction (QEC) and unlock a new age of human progress. From advances in material and climate science, to complex chemistry simulation for new drug design, quantum computers will help humanity solve some of its most important challenges. But without QEC, the industry’s defining technical challenge, such breakthroughs can never be achieved.&amp;nbsp;&amp;nbsp;Riverlane is the world leader in QEC technology. QEC is a complex problem that requires a range of skills,&amp;nbsp;talent&amp;nbsp;and passion.&amp;nbsp;&lt;/span&gt;&lt;span data-ccp-props=&quot;{&amp;quot;201341983&amp;quot;:0,&amp;quot;335559740&amp;quot;:276}&quot;&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/p&gt;

&lt;p&gt;&lt;strong&gt;&lt;span data-contrast=&quot;auto&quot;&gt;What you will do &lt;/span&gt;&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;span data-ccp-props=&quot;{}&quot;&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;As a Quantum Scientist, you will work within Riverlane’s Research team, supporting our partners as they design, run and interpret quantum computing experiments, while also contributing to Riverlane’s internal research in QEC.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;Working with a diverse set of partners, you will gain exposure to different qubit architectures and develop an understanding of their respective QEC considerations. Using your knowledge of quantum computing, you will help translate experimental goals into practical implementations, analyse results, and share insights that inform both customer success and ongoing product and research development.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;Whilst this is a research-focused role, you will collaborate directly with customers to set up experiments, understand technical challenges, and support progress, as well as work closely with internal teams to resolve issues and advance projects. You will also represent Riverlane externally through conference attendance, presentations, and technical discussions.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;Strong communication skills, a collaborative mindset, and an interest in applying research in real-world settings are essential for success in this role.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;In this role, you will:&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;ul&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Work with partners across the UK and Europe to design and execute quantum computing experiments, using Riverlane’s software and tools&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Support the deployment of quantum applications and QEC-related solutions into partner environments&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Investigate experimental results, diagnose issues, and work with internal research and product teams to resolve them&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Contribute to internal research projects related to quantum computing and quantum error correction&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Present results internally and externally, including at customer meetings, workshops and conferences&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;/ul&gt;
