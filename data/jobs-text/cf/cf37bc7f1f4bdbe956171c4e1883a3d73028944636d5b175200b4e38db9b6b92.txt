&lt;h3&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Overview&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;
&lt;p&gt;We are looking for an experienced &lt;strong&gt;Technical Authority (TA)&lt;/strong&gt; to provide senior technical leadership on large, multi‑disciplinary &lt;strong&gt;Communications engineering&lt;/strong&gt; programmes. &lt;p&gt;Our projects are complex, technically challenging and commercially significant. You will lead and inspire expert engineering teams, work closely with programme managers and act as a trusted technical partner to clients.&lt;/p&gt;

&lt;li&gt;Applying your experience and sound engineering judgement to manage technical risk, make decisions under pressure, and keep projects on track.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Selecting and tailoring the appropriate development approaches to suit project objectives, risk profiles, and delivery constraints.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;/ul&gt;
&lt;h3&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Project &amp;amp; Commercial Delivery&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;

&lt;/ul&gt;
&lt;h3&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Client Engagement &amp;amp; Development&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;

&lt;li&gt;Providing clear technical direction and communication of project objectives and priorities, while enabling autonomy, innovation, and exploration of complex technical solutions.&lt;/li&gt;
