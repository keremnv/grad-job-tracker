Until now, insurance has been making billion-pound decisions using outdated tools. We’re changing that. Our platform brings together data, AI, and human expertise to give insurers the fastest path from submission to decision, helping them move faster, act smarter, and take on more risk with confidence.

Backed by a16z, Highland Europe, and Battery Ventures, we’re scaling globally. We’re already trusted by nearly 50 of the world’s largest insurers, with zero churn and billions in premiums flowing through hx.

What began as a single product in one market has rapidly evolved into a multi-product, multi-territory platform powering every stage of pricing and underwriting. AI is at the core of what we do, from building the world’s first domain-specific AI peer programmer for insurance (think GitHub Copilot with a PhD in actuarial science) to shaping agentic workflows that reinvent how this industry operates.

What makes hx different is the people who build it. Here, impact isn’t tied to title or tenure. It’s defined by the challenges you take on and the discipline you bring. Surrounded by peers who stretch you, you’ll do the best, hardest work of your life in a company engineered to endure.

If that sounds like you, join us in building what comes next.




ABOUT THE DELIVERY ENGINEERING TEAM

The hyperexponential Delivery Engineering team is a unique blend of engineers, actuaries, mathematicians and data scientists. We don’t take on traditional “transformations” (we have brilliant partners for that). We specialise in handling only the most difficult and interesting problems our customers face, where only a combination of technical depth, market expertise and hx knowledge will do.

The Delivery Engineering team are the experts' experts of the hx platform, knowing it top-to-bottom, inside-out – both breadth and depth. You’ll learn its limits and which can be pushed. You’ll uncover every trick in the book, the secret combinations, and the shortcuts. You’ll combine all of this with a growing knowledge of insurance technology to present 'the art of the possible' to our customers and help shape cutting-edge tech, while working alongside some of the brightest, most ambitious people in the tech and insurance industries.




WHAT YOU'LL BE DOING

 - Move customer teams from hx-led build to independent delivery, shifting effort from pairing toward review and advisory.

 - Keep every engagement moving to its prescribed path, sequencing services to each customer's build stage.

 - Deliver structured enablement sessions; architecture, coding best practice, testing and deployment, model lifecycles.

 - Run code review as a consistent, standards-anchored service, evolving hx's model development standards where reviews expose gaps.

 - Provide drop-in support shaped by each customer's build stage, resolving most issues in session rather than escalating.

 - Make Developer+ repeatable rather than personality-dependent, documenting delivery and using AI to scale capacity.

 - Convert delivery knowledge into hx Academy content, prioritised by what reviews and drop-ins actually surface.

 - Act as the technical voice of the customer into Product, feeding back recurring friction on a regular rhythm.




WHAT YOU'LL NEED TO HAVE DONE

 - Shipped and maintained production-grade Python code in complex or data-heavy environments, including testing, deployment and model lifecycles.

 - Have excellent pricing and modelling judgement when working with customer’s - working with insurance & actuarial teams is a plus.

 - Developed strong technical enablement instincts, pitching sessions and documentation at the audience's real level.

 - Coached customer teams to work independently rather than solving problems for them.

 - Operated with high ownership and self-direction, building repeatable process where none exists.

 - Prioritised transparently across competing delivery, review and content demands.

 - Communicated with credibility and clarity across developers, actuaries, delivery partners and internal stakeholders.
