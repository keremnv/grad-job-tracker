&lt;h3&gt;&lt;strong&gt;&lt;span data-contrast=&quot;auto&quot;&gt;About us&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;span data-ccp-props=&quot;{}&quot;&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;
&lt;p&gt;&lt;span data-contrast=&quot;none&quot;&gt;Riverlane’s&amp;nbsp;mission is to master quantum error correction (QEC) and unlock a new age of human progress. From advances in material and climate science, to complex chemistry simulation for new drug design, quantum computers will help humanity solve some of its most important challenges. But without QEC, the industry’s defining technical challenge, such breakthroughs can never be achieved.&amp;nbsp;&amp;nbsp;Riverlane is the world leader in QEC technology. QEC is a complex problem that requires a range of skills,&amp;nbsp;talent&amp;nbsp;and passion.&amp;nbsp;&lt;/span&gt;&lt;span data-ccp-props=&quot;{&amp;quot;201341983&amp;quot;:0,&amp;quot;335559740&amp;quot;:276}&quot;&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/p&gt;

&lt;h3&gt;&lt;strong&gt;About our new Maryland office&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;
&lt;p&gt;We are opening an office in Maryland to work more closely with the US government agencies, national laboratories and research institutions that are shaping the future of quantum computing. This role is one of our founding hires there. You will help establish Riverlane&#39;s presence in one of the densest concentrations of quantum research in the world, and have a direct hand in how we build the team, the partnerships and the programs that follow.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;h3&gt;&lt;strong&gt;&lt;span data-contrast=&quot;auto&quot;&gt;About the role&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;strong&gt; &lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;
&lt;p&gt;&lt;span data-ccp-props=&quot;{}&quot;&gt;Riverlane’s overarching mission is making quantum computing useful sooner. To achieve this goal we need to provide future developers with tools and frameworks that enable great science. As for-profit company, open-source is an investment in our future: we want to grow the quantum community, help them learn this nascent technology and evolve it together. In this role, you will set our direction in this space, driving our open-source efforts, identifying the components that can be used by the community, building them either from zero or by adapting our internal toolboxes and finally disseminating their value.&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;h3&gt;&lt;strong&gt;&lt;span data-contrast=&quot;auto&quot;&gt;What you will do &lt;/span&gt;&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;
&lt;p&gt;&lt;span data-ccp-props=&quot;{}&quot;&gt;You will be responsible for &lt;a href=&quot;https://www.riverlane.com/deltakit&quot;&gt;Deltakit&lt;/a&gt;, our open SDK, driving the teams that develop analysis tools, simulators, visualizers and core components. You will use your technical expertise to set priorities and directions in collaboration with our product team and in line with our business model, effectively shaping and building our products. You will interface with the internal developer community to understand and support them, as well as the global community. Finally, you will identify opportunities to amplify internal efforts and build software that is widely accessible to the global ecosystem.&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/p&gt;
