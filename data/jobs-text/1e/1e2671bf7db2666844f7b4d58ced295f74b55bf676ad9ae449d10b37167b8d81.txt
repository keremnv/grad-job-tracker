As a People Experience Advisor, you will provide practical, accurate and employee-focused support across the employee lifecycle. Acting as a first point of contact for managers and employees, you will advise on people policies and processes, manage routine and moderately complex employee relations matters, and help ensure a consistent, high-quality service.
You will work closely with the wider People Experience team, triaging casework and escalating complex, high-risk or business-critical matters to the People Experience Partner. You will also contribute to people projects, policy development and continuous improvement activity

<div>
<p>· Provide timely and accurate advice to employees and managers on people policies, procedures and day-to-day queries.</p>
<p>· Triage employee relations cases, independently managing lower to moderate-risk matters and escalating complex cases appropriately.</p>
<p>· Support formal processes including disciplinary, grievance, absence, probation and performance matters through effective coordination, documentation and guidance.</p>
<p>· Maintain accurate case records and employee data in line with privacy, confidentiality and data-management requirements.</p>
<p>· Support employee lifecycle activity, including onboarding, offboarding, contractual changes and benefits or payroll coordination where required.</p>
<p>· Coordinate client vetting requirements, monitor progress and support the delivery of relevant service measures.</p>
<p>· Support policy reviews, research, communications and the implementation of process or legislative changes.</p>
<p>· Contribute to People Experience projects focused on employee engagement, experience and operational improvement.</p>
<p>· Work collaboratively within the people experience team and with wider business stakeholders to support team and organisational priorities.</p>
</div>
<div>
<p>· Experience in an HR advisory or generalist people role.</p>
<p>· A working knowledge of UK employment law and HR practice.</p>
<p>· A CIPD qualification at Level 3 or above.</p>
<p>· Confidence advising employees and managers, with the judgement to recognise when escalation is required.</p>
<p>· Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with a clear and professional approach.</p>
<p>· Strong organisation, planning and prioritisation skills, with the ability to manage a varied workload.</p>
<p>· Excellent attention to detail and the ability to handle sensitive information appropriately.</p>
<p>· Effective stakeholder-management and interpersonal skills.</p>
<p>· Confidence using Microsoft Office applications, including Word and Excel.</p>
<p>· The ability to work with complexity and nuance while maintaining a positive employee experience.</p>
</div>
