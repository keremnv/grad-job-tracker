&lt;h3&gt;&lt;strong&gt;⭐ About the role&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;
&lt;p&gt;We&#39;re looking for a hands-on, detail-oriented and proactive &lt;strong&gt;Operational Tax Senior Manager&lt;/strong&gt; to support the Tax team at Monzo, and our wider Finance and Product teams.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;You&#39;ll be joining our Tax Team and will be leading on all taxes that touch our customers and their tax reporting, across the UK and Europe. You’ll report into our Tax Governance Lead.&amp;nbsp;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;This role covers all taxes that relate to customer liabilities or customer tax reporting. These are a core part of product design: we make sure we comply with our own obligations, and we make it as easy as possible for customers to comply with theirs. As we launch new products and enter new markets, this is a key area of our tax strategy, and this role gives you the exciting opportunity to own it and work closely with diverse teams across the business.&amp;nbsp;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;h3&gt;&lt;strong&gt;🔑 You’ll play a key role by…&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;
&lt;ul&gt;
&lt;li&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Owning operational tax:&lt;/strong&gt; Be Monzo’s subject matter expert on operational tax, advising the business and making sure we meet our obligations as we grow and enter new markets.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Leading compliance &amp;amp; controls: &lt;/strong&gt;Own our operational tax processes and controls across jurisdictions, including BBSI, ISA reporting, FATCA and CRS, Corporate Criminal Offence and CT61s, plus overseas equivalents. Build new processes and controls as our products and markets evolve.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Shaping new products:&lt;/strong&gt; Partner with Product and local market teams from the outset to embed tax into product design, build the right reporting and controls, and pressure-test edge cases.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Staying ahead of change:&lt;/strong&gt; Keep on top of tax laws, regulations and case law across the UK and EU, translating changes into clear actions and helping Monzo prepare for new requirements.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Partnering across Monzo: &lt;/strong&gt;Work closely with Product, Financial Crime, Engineering, Legal, EU Next Markets and Finance to solve problems and support business initiatives from an operational tax perspective.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;/ul&gt;
