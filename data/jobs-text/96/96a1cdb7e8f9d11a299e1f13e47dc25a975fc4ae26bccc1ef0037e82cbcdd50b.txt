&lt;h3&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Overview&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;
&lt;p&gt;We are looking for a commercially minded Human Resources Business Partner to join our People Team on a 12-month fixed-term maternity cover basis.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;This is a broad and varied HR generalist role, coaching managers and employees across the full employee lifecycle. You will be a trusted advisor to people managers, providing pragmatic guidance on employee relations, people management and talent development.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;Alongside delivering excellent HR practice, you&#39;ll help us become a more data-led People function. Working with our existing systems and tools, you&#39;ll identify opportunities to improve processes, enhance reporting, and explore practical ways AI can support efficiency, insight and informed choices across HR activities.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;You should be someone who enjoys working things out, is comfortable with ambiguity and likes finding smarter ways to get things done. You’ll bring a proactive and adaptable approach, build strong relationships across the business, and feel comfortable influencing and supporting others when it matters.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;We offer flexible working, with the expectation that you’ll spend at least three days a week in the office. Depending on the work and projects you’re involved in, there may also be times you’ll be required to be in the office everyday.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;h3&gt;&lt;strong&gt;What you’ll be doing&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;
&lt;p&gt;Your role will be varied but we have listed below some of the things you can expect to be responsible for:&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;ul&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Partner with managers in defined business areas to deliver effective people solutions that support business objectives.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Provide expert advice and guidance on all aspects of employee relations, including absence, performance management, disciplinary, grievance, conduct and probation matters.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Coach and develop managers to build confidence and capability in people management.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Support organisational change processes where required.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Contribute to talent management, succession planning and employee development initiatives.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Support annual HR activities, including pay reviews, promotions and performance cycles.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Monitor, analyse and interpret people data to provide meaningful insights and recommendations.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Explore practical applications of AI and technology to improve productivity, reporting and the employee experience.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;/ul&gt;
&lt;p&gt;Contribute to People Team projects and continuous improvement initiatives.&lt;/p&gt;

&lt;ul&gt;

&lt;li&gt;Communication, stakeholder management and relationship-building skills.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;A proactive approach, with the confidence to work independently and drive improvements.&lt;/li&gt;
