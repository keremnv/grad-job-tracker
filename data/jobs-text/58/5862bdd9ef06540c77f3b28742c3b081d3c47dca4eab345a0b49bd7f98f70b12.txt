&lt;p&gt;&lt;strong&gt;&lt;span data-contrast=&quot;auto&quot;&gt;About us&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;span data-ccp-props=&quot;{}&quot;&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;&lt;span data-contrast=&quot;none&quot;&gt;Riverlane’s&amp;nbsp;mission is to master quantum error correction (QEC) and unlock a new age of human progress. From advances in material and climate science, to complex chemistry simulation for new drug design, quantum computers will help humanity solve some of its most important challenges. But without QEC, the industry’s defining technical challenge, such breakthroughs can never be achieved.&amp;nbsp;&amp;nbsp;Riverlane is the world leader in QEC technology. QEC is a complex problem that requires a range of skills,&amp;nbsp;talent&amp;nbsp;and passion.&amp;nbsp;&lt;/span&gt;&lt;span data-ccp-props=&quot;{&amp;quot;201341983&amp;quot;:0,&amp;quot;335559740&amp;quot;:276}&quot;&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/p&gt;

&lt;p&gt;&lt;strong&gt;&lt;span data-contrast=&quot;auto&quot;&gt;About the role&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;strong&gt;&lt;span data-contrast=&quot;none&quot;&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;We are looking for an exceptional Senior Product Manager with a background in science or engineering and quantum computing or HPC experience to help define and grow an important part of Riverlane’s software product portfolio.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;This is an early-stage, fast-moving area of our product roadmap: you’ll help shape its direction as much as run what already exists.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;You’ll sit right at the heart of this product area, working closely with users, partners and cross-functional teams to deeply understand real-world challenges and translate them into a compelling product direction. You’ll use a blend of user insight, product data and market context to cut through complexity and focus on what matters most.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;You’ll own and evolve the strategy and roadmap, bringing together engineering, research, business development and marketing around a shared vision, striking the balance between bold, long-term ambition and what’s realistically achievable today.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;This role sits close to the centre of Riverlane, working daily with colleagues across Engineering, Science and the PMO. We ask for three days a week in our Cambridge office to make that collaboration easy.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;This role involves quarterly travel, less than 10% of your time, to conferences such as IEEE and to visit customers.&lt;/p&gt;

&lt;li&gt;Engage with customers, partners and internal teams to build a complete picture of market needs and the competitive landscape&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Use product usage data, market insight and competitive analysis to prioritise features and drive adoption&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Align stakeholders across the C-suite, research, engineering, business development and marketing, and identify new market opportunities for Riverlane&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Support go-to-market execution and launch readiness with Marketing and Business Development, including external collateral and documentation&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Monitor delivery progress and adapt plans as new challenges or opportunities emerge&lt;/li&gt;
