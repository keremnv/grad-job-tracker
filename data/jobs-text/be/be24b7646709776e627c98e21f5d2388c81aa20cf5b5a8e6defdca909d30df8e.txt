A World-Changing Company
 
Palantir builds the world’s leading software for data-driven decisions and operations. By bringing the right data to the people who need it, our platforms empower our partners to develop lifesaving drugs, forecast supply chain disruptions, locate missing children, and more.


The Role
As a Data Engineer, you will help to build and maintain the data foundations, reporting workflows, and automations that support Recruiting and Hiring Operations. This role will work cross-functionally with Hiring Operations, Recruiters, Data, Security, Privacy, and other technical and non technical partners to turn complex recruiting processes into reliable datasets, dashboards, and operational tools. The ideal candidate enjoys working at the intersection of data engineering and business operations. You will be expected to understand how recruiting workflows generate data and build practical solutions that improve day to day operations.

This role is well suited for someone who can move fluidly between building a pipeline, debugging an integration, creating a dashboard, documenting an operating procedure, and helping stakeholders understand how to use the resulting tool.

<div>

<li>Build, maintain, and improve datasets and pipelines supporting Recruiting</li>
<li>Integrate data from systems such as Lever, Workday, Foundry, and other recruiting systems</li>
<li>Develop reporting datasets and dashboards for recruiting performance, candidate pipelines, recruiting programs, and operational audits</li>
<li>Evaluate existing data integrations for opportunities to reduce latency and implement necessary changes</li>
<li>Partner with central Data and platform teams on warehouse connections, schema changes, API behavior, and source-system migrations</li>
<li>Support integrations between recruiting systems and internal tools including writeback of data updates</li>
<li>Design data access and reporting solutions that respect confidentiality, role-based permissions, and regional or organizational access boundaries</li>
<li>Create clear documentation for sensitive workflows and ensure that operational users understand the limitations of the data</li>
<li>Proactively identify opportunities to improve reporting and workflows as recruiting processes change</li>
<li>Partner with team members, engineering, product and business leaders to understand current user behavior and patterns across our platform</li>
<li>Monitor pipelines for stability, suggest and implement efficiency initiatives</li>
