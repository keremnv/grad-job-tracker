ABOUT THE PRODUCT MARKETING TEAM

Product Marketing sits at the centre of hx's go-to-market engine, translating what we build into stories the market believes. Nothing earns that belief faster than a customer saying it themselves: insurance is an industry where reputation travels through a small number of trusted voices, and our customers include some of the most respected names in specialty and commercial insurance.

As our Customer Marketing Manager, you'll own that voice. Your mission is to increase the frequency, quality, and number of published customer case studies, build relationships with customer accounts so that every testimonial is genuinely mutually beneficial, find new ways to celebrate our customers, and manage award submissions that earn industry recognition for hx and the customers we serve.

This is a role with real ownership from day one. You'll work directly with senior stakeholders at hx and at some of the world's largest insurers, and the content you produce will be used across sales, marketing, and events globally.




WHAT YOU'LL BE DOING

 - Own case study production end to end - from identifying the story and recruiting the customer through to publication - delivering two published case studies (written, video, or otherwise) per quarter.

 - Publish two additional pieces of customer-contributed content per quarter, from quotes and social content to event appearances, driving hx's key messages to market through customer voices.

 - Build and continually improve our case study recruitment and production process so that participating is low-lift for both our customers and our team, and each cycle runs smoother than the last.

 - Grow engagement across the underwriting community, measured through event attendance, email open rates, and social engagement, particularly on LinkedIn.

 - Identify and manage award submissions throughout the year, coordinating internal and customer stakeholders to produce a targeted number of high-quality entries that increase industry recognition for hx and its customers.

 - Edit and assemble short-form written, video, and social content for promotion and lead generation, keeping every asset aligned with hx's positioning and brand narrative.




WHAT YOU'LL NEED TO HAVE DONE

 - Taken customer stories or content projects from initial idea through to publication, coordinating multiple senior, time-limited stakeholders inside and outside your organisation.

 - Built content programmes or campaigns where the relationship mattered as much as the output - securing participation, managing approvals and consent, and leaving customers glad they took part.

 - Produced short-form written and video content yourself, with the functional writing and editing skills to create new assets and make light edits without waiting on others.

 - Grown an audience or generated leads through social content, particularly by editing and assembling content for LinkedIn.

 - Demonstrated strong instincts for positioning and messaging - shaping stories that align with a brand narrative rather than just documenting what happened.

 - Improved a process so it lasted: found the friction, fixed it, and made the next cycle measurably easier for everyone involved.




YOU'RE UNLIKELY TO THRIVE HERE IF

 - You prefer being handed a finished brief over hunting down a story, winning people over, and solving problems as you find them.

 - You see customers primarily as a source of quotes rather than partners whose goals, reputation, and time deserve as much care as our own.

 - You're uncomfortable coordinating multiple senior stakeholders with competing priorities and limited availability.

 - If reading our Culture Document https://hx.wiki/ leaves you feeling neutral rather than energised, hx may not be the place where you'll do your best work. We're building something that asks for commitment and conviction, and we want you to feel excited by the opportunity to grow with us.
