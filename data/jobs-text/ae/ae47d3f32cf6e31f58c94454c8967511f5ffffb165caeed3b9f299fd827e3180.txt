&lt;p&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Engineering at Monzo&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;We have 500+ engineers across Monzo who design and build our in-house banking platform. We have big ambitions for the future, and technology plays a big role in creating a bank our customers want, so engineers at Monzo collaborate across disciplines to solve interesting challenges throughout the company.&lt;/p&gt;

&lt;p&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Engineering Interns&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;As a software engineer intern at Monzo, you’ll be matched with a team where you’ll spend the summer working on a real project for our customers. We’ll pair you with a mentor who will support you to design and ship a solution to solve a real problem.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;You’ll have the opportunity to work alongside engineers to learn more about software engineering and the types of challenges engineers at Monzo solve on a day-to-day basis.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;You’ll spend your internship:&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;ul&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Writing, testing and shipping code that supports new features for our customers&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Reviewing code for your colleagues, to pick up any improvements and learn from your peers&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Working with product managers, designers and data analysts on your team&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Learning about new technologies, engineering practices and what a full-time software engineering role looks like&lt;/li&gt;
