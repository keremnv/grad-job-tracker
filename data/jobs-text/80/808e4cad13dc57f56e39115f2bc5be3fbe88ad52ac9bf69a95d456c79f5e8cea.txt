&lt;p class=&quot;isSelectedEnd&quot;&gt;From day one, you’ll be part of our multidisciplinary medical development team, tackling real technical problems while receiving guidance and training from experienced engineers. You will be part of a team developing&lt;strong&gt; &lt;/strong&gt;technologies that can make a meaningful difference to people’s lives.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p class=&quot;isSelectedEnd&quot;&gt;During your internship, you&#39;ll likely:&amp;nbsp;&lt;/p&gt;

&lt;li&gt;Contribute to a wide range of projects that span the full software development lifecycle.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Collaborate with a multidisciplinary and supportive team, gaining exposure to systems involving electronics, optics, fluidics, robotics, and user-interface components.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Work with industry best practices such as continuous integration, automated testing and analysis, and model-based systems engineering.&lt;/li&gt;
