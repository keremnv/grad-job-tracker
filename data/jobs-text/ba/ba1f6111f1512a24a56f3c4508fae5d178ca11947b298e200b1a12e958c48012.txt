ABOUT THE MODEL AUTHORING TEAM

Model Authoring owns the products our customers use to write and manage their pricing models: the model developer experience and our actuarial agent. An actuary might start with an Excel-based rater and finish with a Python model that has been reviewed, approved and is pricing live business. Our job is to make every step of that credible enough to trust. The team is distributed across our Warsaw and London offices.

As the Staff Product Engineer, you will help set the team's technical direction. This is an individual contributor role, but influence is a large part of it: you'll help define the standards the team works to, raise the technical bar across pods, and own the interfaces where our problems meet those of teams elsewhere in Technology. You'll be expected to move fast and start shaping technical conversations early. We work in an AI-native way, and you'll help define and evolve what that means here.




WHAT YOU'LL BE DOING

 - Establish a set of engineering standards across the Model Authoring pods. The standards are documented and actively referenced in code review, with new pod work conforming by default.

 - Set and own the technical direction for the team's shared enablers, including how we represent and persist models, the model registry, secure context access, and shared agentic infrastructure.

 - Establish the technical interfaces between Model Authoring and other parts of the business. Dependencies with other teams are agreed, documented, and progressing without needing to be brokered by someone else.

 - Work hands-on on the hardest problems in the stream. This is not an advisory role: we would expect you in the code where the risk is highest.

 - Help define how we work in an AI-native way. Which parts of model development and of our own engineering we hand to agents, what stays human, and what evidence we need before trusting either.

 - Visibly lift the engineers around you. You're sought out for design input across pods, and there's at least one engineer whose growth is clearly attributable to your support.




WHAT YOU'LL NEED TO HAVE DONE

 - Worked in an AI-native environment, and genuinely want to keep doing so. You've used AI or agentic tools in your own engineering workflow, introduced or experimented with them, and have a real view on what's worked and what hasn't.

 - Been product-focused rather than purely technical. You understand who your users are, where their pain points are, and how your technical decisions connect to commercial outcomes.

 - Set technical direction while navigating genuine ambiguity. A proven track record of owning architecture in a domain with no established playbook, and making clear calls on imperfect information rather than deferring them.

 - Owned a significant technical challenge or service end to end, with the depth to walk through it in detail - the trade-offs, the contracts, the things you'd do differently.

 - Influenced across teams. You can hold a position under challenge, genuinely listen to pushback, and persuade others - not just present your own view and hope it lands.

 - Worked effectively across distributed teams - this role spans our Warsaw and London offices, so you'll need to be comfortable building relationships and influence across both.







YOU'RE UNLIKELY TO THRIVE HERE IF

 - You'd rather focus purely on the technical problem than get close to users, customer pain points, and the commercial implications of what you build.

 - You're not comfortable setting technical direction in ambiguity, or you'd rather wait for an established playbook than make a call on imperfect information.

 - You're not excited about helping define what the future of software engineering looks like and are prepared to embrace tooling and new ways of working to support this.

If reading our Culture Document https://hx.wiki/ leaves you feeling neutral rather than energised, hx may not be the place where you’ll do your best work. We’re building something that asks for commitment and conviction, and we want you to feel excited by the opportunity to grow with us.
