&lt;h3&gt;&lt;strong&gt;&lt;span data-contrast=&quot;auto&quot;&gt;About the role&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;strong&gt;&lt;span data-contrast=&quot;none&quot;&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/span&gt;&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;
&lt;p&gt;&lt;span data-contrast=&quot;auto&quot;&gt;Reporting to the Chief Commercial Officer, the successful candidate will be responsible for growing Riverlane’s business with the U.S. Department of Defense (DoD) and DoD agencies and laboratories.
